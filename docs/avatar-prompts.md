## Prompt executat per a la composició col·lectiva

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide hero illustration for the Catalan band SDP509. Use the FIRST of the four user reference photographs (eight friends against a pale wall) as the subject reference. The other photos are mood references only. Create exactly eight original collectible vinyl figures representing the eight visible people, keeping visible hair, facial hair, glasses and clothing distinctions from that first photograph. Do not name the people. Oversized rounded heads, small full bodies, premium warm matte vinyl, nuanced sculpted faces, original independent designer toy language, not Funko. Arrange the eight friends closely together in a lively two-depth group with all faces visible, standing on a subtle cream oval base. Mediterranean indie-pop art direction. Soft studio light, warm cream solid background #f6f2e8 matching a paper website. Wide landscape composition, entire group and all feet in frame, modest margins, no cropped people. Clothing simplified with no commercial logos or text. Tiny pineapple leaf sculptural accent on the base. No packaging, no watermark, no lettering, no names. Output a polished usable website asset.
